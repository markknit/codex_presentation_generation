Six separate 80-minute lesson decks follow the V2 sub-strand 1.2 sequence and the Cell Structure v2 visual frame. These are **drafts for review, not approved classroom materials**. The user asked for this six-lesson unit before the earlier pilot had external sign-off; that is draft-generation authorisation, not teacher approval or release approval.

## Start here

Open the relevant `.pptx` in PowerPoint and use Presenter View. Slides 2–3 are hidden teacher orientation and preparation slides; use the notes on every slide. The PDFs include hidden teacher slides and are for review, not pupil projection. `ChemicalsOfLife_Case_and_Cards.docx`, `ChemicalsOfLife_Investigation_Records.docx`, and `ChemicalsOfLife_L6_Role_Cards.docx` are editable support materials. The adapted five-part final assessment needs additional supervised time. Teacher review notes and per-lesson build logs identify open decisions and corrections.

Before use, a teacher and source owner must review science, source coverage, timings, every build-log item, and the safety, equipment, PPE and waste plans for the demonstration and practicals. Lesson 5 uses human saliva in the source: the school must decide whether and how that route is permitted. Do not assume pooled saliva is safe or use an unapproved replacement. ARES links and local offline access are not verified. Native PowerPoint, Presenter View, licensed Calibri fidelity and teacher approval are outstanding. Local PDF previews substitute Carlito for Calibri.

The source case data and nutrient estimates have not been independently authenticated. Treat them as a supplied classroom case, not a diagnosis or proof of an individual nutrient’s causal contribution. Practical records must reflect what learners actually observe. The source’s stated vitamin C and catalase-related outcomes lack clear corresponding investigations in the six-lesson sequence; the source owner needs to resolve coverage rather than having the builder invent new activities.

## Computing and provenance

The meal picture is an AI-generated contextual illustration, not observational evidence. The exact prompt and source-asset hash are in `assets/chemicals_of_life/provenance.json`. It was generated using the hosted built-in image-generation tool; its model, seed and device were not exposed. CUDA on NVIDIA GB10 processed the illustration and every rendered slide for preview and simple pixel measurements. PowerPoint/DOCX construction, PDF conversion and rasterisation, image encoding, file layout and ZIP assembly use CPU-only code paths. CUDA checks do not establish scientific accuracy, native PowerPoint behaviour or pedagogical quality.

Sources, input hashes, exact slide timing, checks and per-lesson changes are recorded under `qa/` and `build_logs/`. No push, publication or classroom release is authorised by this package.
