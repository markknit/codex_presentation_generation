Science limits: Root hairs form behind the growing root tip. Mature xylem vessel elements lose living contents; not all xylem tissue is dead. Sieve-tube elements are living but lack a nucleus at maturity and depend on companion cells. Sugar transport is source-to-sink, not always downward. Guard-cell shape here is a dicot model, not grass guard-cell anatomy. Dry/humid drawings are hypothetical, not a geographic experiment or actual measured stomata. Seed reserves can support early growth without photosynthesis.
