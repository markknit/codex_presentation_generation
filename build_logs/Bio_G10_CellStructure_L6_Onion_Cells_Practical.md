## Required teacher decisions before use

- Complete the school's practical risk assessment and trial the actual equipment, preparation and focusing method.
- Confirm the iodine product/concentration/SDS, quantities, disposal and individual restrictions. The source does not specify a formulation; this draft supplies no recipe or new concentration.
- The source suggests working without goggles and improvising diluted pharmacy iodine tincture. These are expressly unresolved safety issues, not approved fallbacks. The draft keeps the source's core PPE/handling rules and records the disputed alternatives here and in hidden notes; it does not silently substitute a redesigned practical.
- Confirm the source transparent-ruler method for field diameter on the actual microscope/objective. A numerical example must not stand in for a measured field.
- Confirm realistic visibility and timing in a large class. A fixed four-label requirement must not cause pupils to invent unseen structures.

## Resource and accuracy references

The source's specific preparation still needs local qualified sign-off.

[OpenStax on the plant body](https://openstax.org/books/biology/pages/30-1-the-plant-body) supports the protective epidermal role; [University of Maryland Extension on bulbs](https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/bulbs) distinguishes the fleshy food-storage leaves of a bulb. These checks support the limited tissue-role correction, not a replacement lesson sequence.
