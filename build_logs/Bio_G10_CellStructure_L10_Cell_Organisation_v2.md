Preparation: Per group four: two-sided schematic evidence card, five-cell healing strip and four-column table. Per pupil model and two notes. Board, timer, pens, pre-cut materials. Do not label diagrams as real micrographs; optional authenticated microscopy needs separate teacher provision.

Science limits: Use epidermal and fibroblast pathways through epithelial/connective tissues into skin. Immune cells can migrate between tissues. Platelets are cell fragments. Blood/immune cells help the wound but do not all become epidermis or dermis. The hypodermis is below the skin proper. Skin includes several tissue types; a tissue can contain multiple cell types. The school analogy is limited, not an exact institutional or biological map.
