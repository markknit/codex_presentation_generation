Science limits: A simplified healing sequence has overlapping stages. Platelets form a plug; fibrin is formed from existing plasma fibrinogen through clotting reactions, not woven by ribosomes at the wound. Ribosomes assemble protein precursors in cells. DNA replication precedes division; spindle microtubules segregate chromosomes. Most nucleated somatic cells share essentially the same genome; different gene expression supports differentiation. Mature red cells/platelets lack nuclei and not all nucleated cells have literally identical DNA. Use actual practical observations in assessment; no teacher key is shown to pupils.
